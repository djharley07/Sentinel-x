# SENTINEL X
### AI-Powered Industrial Safety, Maintenance Intelligence & Operational Assistance

**SENTINEL X** is an AI-powered industrial assistance platform designed to help factory workers report equipment problems, verify information, maintain incident histories, and support safer maintenance decisions.

Developed as an independent project for the **Gnani AI Great Indian AI Internship 2026**.

## The Problem

Industrial maintenance teams often face communication barriers, incomplete incident reports, fragmented equipment histories, and delays in escalating critical issues.

SENTINEL X explores how multilingual voice AI, structured reporting, operational memory, and human-in-the-loop workflows can improve industrial maintenance operations.

## Core Capabilities

- **Multilingual Voice Reporting:** Voice-based incident reporting with transcription and review workflows.
- **Transcript Verification:** Workers can approve, correct, or rerecord reports to reduce errors.
- **Operational Memory:** Structured equipment and incident history.
- **REDLINE Escalation:** Critical-incident escalation with administrator oversight.
- **Role-Based Workflows:** Interfaces for workers, supervisors, and administrators.
- **Evidence Detective:** Support for attaching visual evidence to equipment reports.
- **Maintenance Analytics:** Insights into recurring faults, repair activity, and equipment history.
- **AI Assistance:** Context-aware support for industrial troubleshooting and reporting.

*Feature availability and completeness may vary across the current prototype.*

## System Workflow

1. A worker submits a voice-based equipment report.
2. Speech recognition converts the recording into text.
3. The worker verifies or corrects the transcription.
4. The system stores the report and its revision history.
5. Equipment context and AI assistance support further investigation.
6. Critical issues can be escalated for authorized review.
7. Reports and maintenance activity support operational analytics.

## Development Status

SENTINEL X is a prototype under active development. It is not a certified industrial safety system and must not replace qualified personnel, established safety procedures, or emergency protocols.
